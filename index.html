But I twisted your words in anger and ego. And I said things that made you feel like I completely misunderstood you. That hurt you. Deeply. And I am genuinely sorry for that.

None of what I said was true. Not even one bit. It was my jealousy, my frustration, my ego clash, and my anger speaking. Not my heart.

You trusted me enough to share your fears. Your worries. Your anxiety. Your stress. And instead of making you feel heard, I made you feel judged.

I don't want you to ever think 100 times before telling me things. Especially not your fears. Especially not your project stress. Especially not the things that keep you awake.

I WANT to hear them. I WANT to understand them. I WANT to be your safe place. Even when I fail at it sometimes.

You are one of the most hardworking, intelligent, emotionally strong people I know. And I hate that I made you feel otherwise.

I am sorry, GK. Truly.
